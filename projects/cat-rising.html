CAT RISING

Team Project • Platformer • Unity

Cat Rising is a cosy action-adventure platformer where the player takes control of a cat on a mission to transform the creatures of a colourful fantasy world into cats.

Players explore different regions through platforming, combat and puzzles, using claw attacks and yarn-based magic to overcome enemies and transform them into feline versions of themselves.

MY ROLE

Sole Programmer & Project Manager

As the project's sole programmer, I was responsible for implementing all programming across the game, from player controls and gameplay systems to UI, enemy AI, audio, camera behaviour and shaders. I also managed the project, organising weekly sprints and helping the team stay aligned on tasks and goals.
