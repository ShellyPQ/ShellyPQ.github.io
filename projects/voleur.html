VOLEUR!

Team Project • Game Jam • Unity

Voleur! is a fast-paced arcade stealth game where the player races against a one-minute countdown to steal treasures from a museum and escape with the highest score possible.

Players can increase their score multiplier by taking greater risks, including staying in the museum longer and raising the security level, creating a risk-versus-reward challenge between escaping safely and pushing for a higher score.
