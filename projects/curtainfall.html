CURTAINFALL

Team Project • AIE Game Jam • Unity

Curtainfall is a puzzle-platformer set within a theatrical play, where the player guides actor Quintus through a series of staged environments inspired by Italian Commedia dell'Arte.

Wearing his mask allows Quintus to perform on stage and fight other actors, while removing it takes him backstage where he can manipulate stage elements and create new paths through each level.
